# Luxury resources, art, dyes and remedies

Added 2026-11-20. Nothing here is needed to stay alive; all of it costs labour and most of it is found
only in particular country, which is the point.

## Score

`/survival score` lists a society's works and monuments. `/survival dedicate` founds a monument where
the person stands: a statue or four columns, and nine square metres of polished marble or mosaic,
within eight blocks. The stones count at their own worth and a tenth more for each further kind of
work. A monument is counted afresh at every reckoning, so one pulled down counts for nothing, and
monuments must stand more than sixteen blocks apart.

Fever bark halves the harm of a serious infection for half a day and cures nothing.

## Stated simplifications

- Flowers no longer make dye (the vanilla one-flower recipes are struck); mixing dyes still works.
- Thirty-two whelks to a measure of purple is some hundreds of times too generous.
- Alum stands for the whole roast-and-leach of pyritic shale in one kiln firing.
- A statue is 320 hours; a real life-size marble took most of a year.
- Dyed yarn forgets its colour; a tapestry is one item whatever went into it.
- The dye and medicine plants are gathered wild and cannot yet be sown.
