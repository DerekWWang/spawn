Work records persist station/skill/physical requirements and the planned output, preventing reload or definition changes from duplicating inputs or awards. Cancellation and breaking return both reserved primary and secondary inputs; completion consumes them once and awards practice only after bodily costs are saved. Old in-flight reservations retain a grandfathered outcome without retroactive practice.
