## Qube

This is a NeoForge mod for Minecraft 26.3 (mod id `spawn_9b5fbb9`).

- Work through the `qube` CLI in the shell: edit, `qube build`, `qube test`, `qube shot`.
- After `qube shot`, open the contact-sheet images it prints before describing how anything looks.
- Textures go through `qube texture`; one is invisible in game until its `bindings_missing` is empty.
- The `qube` skill has the commands, shot segments and texture rules.
